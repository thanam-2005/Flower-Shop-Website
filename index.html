Frequently Asked Questions

How fast is your same-day flower delivery?

Orders placed before 2:00 PM local time are eligible for express same-day delivery within 2 to 4 hours. Our delivery vehicles are climate-controlled to keep your flowers farm-fresh.

How do I keep my bouquet fresh for longer?

Trim stem ends by 1 inch at a 45-degree angle under water, use lukewarm water in a clean vase, add our complimentary floral nourishment packet, and keep away from direct sunlight.

Can I customize a bouquet for a special occasion?

Yes! Simply contact our artisan florist team via the contact form or WhatsApp. We can customize flower varieties, color palettes, wrapping paper, and add personal message cards.
